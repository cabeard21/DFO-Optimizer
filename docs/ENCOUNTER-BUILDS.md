# Encounter objectives in build, gear and spellbook search

The build service accepts an `encounter` alongside the class families, progression opportunities, physical equipment and spellbook. Its native search prices the actual recipes for each legal equipment combination, checks acquisition against carried gold and evaluates the chosen priority list against the frozen encounter. Every returned witness includes a separate scalar simulation of its HP, SP, damage and deadlines. Checkpoint DPS divides realized enemy HP loss by the full declared horizon in seconds. One common seed or draw tape defines one comparison.

Run `dfo build optimize --request examples/build-encounter-reversal.json` to compare two touch spells with the same starting pool and stationary foe. The 500,000 μs horizon allows one release. Set `encounter.scenario.horizon_us` to `20000000` to evaluate sustained damage with the same resources and draw sequence. The fixed tape makes both the saving throws and failed Disintegrate attempts reproducible. This example describes one finite panel.

## Checkpoints and resources

`encounter.at` defaults to `terminal`. A terminal encounter uses the vendor quotes and available equipment at the final progression checkpoint. Set `at` to `checkpoints` for a series of separated enemy groups. Record the common seed, scenario, and a description of the opportunity in `evidence`. Chained touch encounters require an evidenced `idle_us` of at least 1,400,000 between checkpoints. Death prevents further progression. Previous SP spending reduces the resources available for intervening practice and the next encounter. Skill and XP gains from scenario casting are outside this projection.

Replayed steps report the chosen loadout and priority list, initial and final HP/SP, casts, impacts, kills and resource restoration. Paid acquisitions persist. Current scope supports ordinary Damage Health and Disintegrate recipes or validated saved records. The planner reports gaps for unresolved hostile spell catalogs, self-blast or reflected player damage, inter-checkpoint restoration or timed powers without enough information to reconstruct them. Wellspring uses the equipped physical jewelry roll in a terminal encounter with verified capacity. A validated path requires its permanent mastery pair and an attributed `path_evidence` observation with `source`, `observed_at_ms`, `context: "ordinary_online_pve"` and `initial_state: "fresh"`. This records the cleared combat timers, counters and fractional gains at encounter entry.

## Current-character entry points

`gear optimize`, `gear evaluate`, `spellbook optimize` and `spellbook evaluate` take the same terminal encounter and lifecycle `spellbook` object. Supply `character` and any timestamped `live_observations`, including current and maximum SP. Gear search uses eligible physical items. Gear evaluation and spellbook operations use the observed worn equipment unless the request includes an explicit `loadout`. Fixed spellbook evaluation takes one submitted priority list. `mode: "owned_plus_budget"` admits attributed equipment offers. `budget.gold` caps joint equipment and Spell Maker purchases. These entry points return the lifecycle envelope, accepted by `build replay`.

## Permanent progression

An `oghma` action names a distinct pack copy and supplies either eight attribute increases in source order or an `allocation_order` permutation. Reading consumes the observed Oghma Infinium, distributes the 30-point pool up to the earned attribute cap, then clears the pending level-up dialog. Oghma can be used at level 45. Later resource projections requiring a new live capacity report the missing observation.

A current character's `start.firmament` observation supplies `character_id`, `source`, `observed_at_ms`, `access`, `tree`, `renown` and ten `profession_ranks`. Each `firmament` action specifies its exact prior tree in `from` and one new sign or star in `node`. A `goal.firmament` names the desired final tree. Search verifies graph connectivity, terminal keystones, craft points and combat stars. Respec silver and earning new account entitlements require further scope. The source locations for the additive law are `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js`, `firmamentTree.js`, `src/systems/artifactEffects.js` and `src/systems/advancement.js` in the pinned DFO checkout.

Encounter builds can score a funded tree's static spell power, pierce, cast speed and flat Destruction bonus. Supply `encounter.firmament_evidence` with a timestamp, source, `context: "ordinary_online_pve"`, `in_force: true` and `mentor_scale: 1`. For a tree with pool modifiers, add `sp_capacity_basis: "observed_with_tree"` and provide current and maximum SP observations at the tree's timestamp. Changes that alter capacity need another observation. Unsupported keystones and other unimplemented channels identify the affected checkpoint.

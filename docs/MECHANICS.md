## Regression evidence

The 14 public Dhargo class files independently parse at −12 and 0.30× with zero skipped picks, 12 skills and 400 attribute points. One additional constructive witness pairs HP 30 (+22) and 3× INT Magery (+10) with prohibitions on steel, elven, dwarven, mithril, silver, adamantium and iron (−44). These seven restrictions consume the entire disadvantage allowance and must follow the class into future gear evaluation. [Dhargo comparison corpus](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1GNvjKwC8XU5wXPA3VMkdWFNp3efRr8wb)

## Open mechanics

M2 verifies immediate Damage Health for an ordinary PvE target, and M3 applies the supported equipment channels to that calculation. The coverage registry records the available item sources and the assumptions required for each. Encounter survival, general combat regeneration and conditional powers remain open. ## Online birth and advancement

The online character-creation path grants skill-based starter items and spells. A qualifying seed-42 source fixture starts with 33 HP, 27 SP, 27 gold and a birth skill sum of 154. Minor Shock and Arcane Arrow arrive as complete spell records in that example. Classic stock IDs require a local record for evaluating their effects. Starting damage also depends on spellbook possession and sufficient current SP. The creator's base spell-set choice alone cannot establish it. [Independent source fixture](../tests/fixtures/progression-oracle.json)

Progression distinguishes a skill tally, the next qualifying raise check, an earned level and claiming its attributes. The source uses the birth sum and high-water sum for leveling, individual skill categories for mastery and separate checks for selecting a specialization and purchasing its signature. Rest, recent use, challenge state and training prices have distinct clocks and resource requirements. The creation-to-cap example assumes repeated access to eligible activities and safe beds. It supplies a conditional reachability witness for the named class. [Creation-to-cap workload](../examples/progression-creation-to-cap.json) · [Earned mastery example](../examples/progression-mastery.json)

The spellbook evaluator supports one to three ordered slots of Damage Health and Disintegrate. Within each cast the second effect sees the HP left by the first. Between casts the scenario supplies a fresh, stationary ordinary target. The native evaluator and an independent scalar replay check the final policies against the same finite recipes, SP and acquisition assumptions. Live opponents, boss behavior, sustained recovery and arbitrary signature effects still lack verified encounter models. [Game-derived cast vectors](../tests/fixtures/spellbook-oracle.json)

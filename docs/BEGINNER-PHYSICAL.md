# Ordinary physical encounters

The beginner's physical scenarios use the pinned online Physical Combat and Armor Overhaul settings. They compare a stationary caster with awake, facing human class opponents. The encounter request specifies when each enemy attempts a swing, its position, skill, equipment and level. These are authored opportunities. The engine resolves the body location, hit, critical, damage and wear on the shared trial draw stream. A missed or out-of-reach attack can leave HP unchanged. [Pinned attack resolver](https://github.com/Lattymoy/daggerfall-js-source/blob/a3eb1605ea1dc391b691fe314377458a7e63e483/src/combat/pcaao.js#L1614-L1860)

The defender's live attributes and Dodging affect the result. Plate, chain, leather, footwear and a shield use the piece covering the struck location, its material and remaining condition. Shield block uses its covered locations, a weaker off-location chance and a comparison with the armor beneath. The active online overhaul reduces damage through armor. It also reduces the damage of a weapon below the target's required metal. The encounter retains each piece's condition independently between swings and rooms. Broken equipment loses its contribution. A broken main-hand weapon leaves bare-hand attacks available. [Armor reductions](https://github.com/Lattymoy/daggerfall-js-source/blob/a3eb1605ea1dc391b691fe314377458a7e63e483/src/combat/pcaao.js#L1138-L1430) · [Equipment wear](https://github.com/Lattymoy/daggerfall-js-source/blob/a3eb1605ea1dc391b691fe314377458a7e63e483/src/combat/formulas.js#L569-L596)

The fallback uses a sideways main-hand swing when the actor cannot start a spell from the declared priority list and a living target is in reach. The current weapon, Speed, Strength and frame interval determine its hit and recovery times. A spell and a swing occupy the same action clock. The scenario assumes an already equipped weapon, a facing player and no inventory changes during combat. A swing spends eight fatigue at its hit frame. The policy preserves positive fatigue and stops attempting swings when the next one could cause exhaustion. Standing still on the ground incurs no movement-band fatigue. Skill practice during a fight provides no immediate skill raise in these checkpoint trials. [Swing schedule](https://github.com/Lattymoy/daggerfall-js-source/blob/a3eb1605ea1dc391b691fe314377458a7e63e483/src/characters/weaponStates.js#L413-L435) · [Hit-frame debit](https://github.com/Lattymoy/daggerfall-js-source/blob/a3eb1605ea1dc391b691fe314377458a7e63e483/src/scenes/dungeonContext.js#L7695-L7705)

The trial reports melee damage, attempts, hits, incoming attacks, ending fatigue and gross fatigue spent. It retains HP, SP, fatigue and wear between rooms. A protected cast uses the owned spell and pays its price. A new checkpoint starts a fresh counterfactual encounter with its own declared state. Monster natural attack schedules, weapon mastery riders, martial techniques, archery, poison, enchanted weapon procs, PvP and live-world availability require additional evidence. The ordinary weapon lane accepts supported mundane templates with known current and maximum condition. The trace's melee impact includes the body-part index in its fourth element, from head at zero through feet at six. [Compiled resolver](../src/dfo_optimizer/physical_combat.hpp) · [Independent replay](../src/dfo_optimizer/physical_reference.py)

An older weapon can still carry the durability pool from its original template. On its first damaging contact, the game migrates that pool before subtracting wear. The model preserves the worn share and uses the resulting pool for subsequent blows, including across room transitions. Swing timing uses the weapon weights from the active Roleplay & Realism item templates.

DFO class · Breton
Character creation and development checklist
The development example uses paired birth seed 42.

[ ] Choose your race
    Select Breton in character creation. Race is a separate choice when importing the class file.
    The online creator goes directly from class to name.
    Select Average reflexes to match the tested character.

[ ] Set your class attributes
    Strength 40, Intelligence 75, Willpower 30, Agility 75, Endurance 20, Personality 40, Speed 60, Luck 60.
    Set class health to 30 HP per level. Birth rolls and bonuses determine your starting sheet.

[ ] Enter the ordered skill groups
    Primary skills in order — Illusion → Alteration → Thaumaturgy
    Major skills in order — Destruction → Axe → Restoration
    Minor skills in order — Dodging → Swimming → Short Blade → Etiquette → Dragonish → Mysticism

[ ] Select advantages and disadvantages
    Advantages — Acute hearing, Adrenaline rush, Bonus to hit Humanoid, Expertise in Blunt weapon, Expertise in Short blade, Immunity To fire, Increased magery Int in spell points3.
    Disadvantages — Forbidden armor type Chain, Forbidden armor type Leather, Forbidden material Dwarven, Forbidden material Ebony, Forbidden material Mithril, Forbidden shield types Kite shield, Forbidden shield types Tower shield.
    Magery gives this class 3 spell points per point of Intelligence before other modifiers.

[ ] Spend your birth bonus
    Allocate up to four points to Endurance, then Intelligence, Speed and Willpower within legal caps. Explicit allocations override that rule.
    Spend all six points per group on its lowest rolled skills.
    The replay used Intelligence +9, Endurance +4.
    The primary bonus on this example roll went to Illusion +3, Alteration +3.
    The major bonus on this example roll went to Destruction +2, Axe +1, Restoration +3.
    The minor bonus on this example roll went to Dodging +2, Swimming +1, Short Blade +2, Etiquette +1.

[ ] Play around each disadvantage
    Forbidden armor type · Chain. You cannot wear chain armor.
    Forbidden armor type · Leather. You cannot wear leather armor.
    Forbidden material · Dwarven. You cannot equip items made of dwarven. Check the material before buying an upgrade.
    Forbidden material · Ebony. You cannot equip items made of ebony. Check the material before buying an upgrade.
    Forbidden material · Mithril. You cannot equip items made of mithril. Check the material before buying an upgrade.
    Forbidden shield types · Kite shield. You cannot equip the kite shield shield type.
    Forbidden shield types · Tower shield. You cannot equip the tower shield shield type.

[ ] Choose your opening spells
    Your starting spellbook contains Arcane Arrow, Gentle Fall, Candle, Salve Bruise, Rise.
    The example birth has 252 SP available before the opening fight.
    Arcane Arrow costs 33 SP per cast on this roll.
    Use Arcane Arrow [991] at range for offense.
    Use Salve Bruise [995] to restore health. The scenario policy checks healing below 60% health.

[ ] Equip your first loadout
    Equip the starting copies used in the replay. Right hand — War Axe, Chest clothes — Short Shirt, Legs clothes — Casual Pants, Cloak2 — Formal Cloak, Feet — Shoes.
    When spell points run low, use a proficient backup from the starting kit. The checked copies include Steel War Axe (Axe), Iron Dagger (Short Blade).
    The carry test uses a hypothetical Iron Cuirass. It weighs 12.5 kg.
    The starting kit plus that cuirass leaves at least 21.3 kg free across the tested births.
    The level-15 replay acquired Iron plate cuirass for 30 gold under the declared offer.

[ ] Plan your first levels
    At level-up, allocate attributes in this order. Endurance, Intelligence, Speed, Willpower, Strength, Agility, Personality, Luck.
    Practice the skills on the route, starting with Illusion, Alteration, Thaumaturgy.
    At the first earned level (2), the replay gained Endurance +5.
    The next encounter checkpoint was level 15.
    On this roll the checkpoint required 1,145,040 in-game minutes under the supplied opportunities.
    Start with the actual birth spellbook. Pay for missing practice recipes during training. At the first scored earned level, buy the stated area-touch recipe at the ordinary Spell Maker price if affordable after armor and obligatory training.
    The replay assumes one qualifying action every 60 seconds and access to full bed rests.

[ ] Plan your masteries
    Prioritize mastery in this order. Illusion, Alteration, Thaumaturgy, Destruction, Restoration, Mysticism.
    Mastered at the final checkpoint — Destruction, Restoration, Illusion, Alteration, Thaumaturgy, Mysticism.
    Primary school choices include Illusion, Alteration, Thaumaturgy.
    Major school choices include Destruction, Restoration.
    Minor school choices include Mysticism.

[ ] Choose an arcane specialization
    Leave the path open at first. The replay measured this build before choosing a specialization.
    If melee enemies repeatedly reach you and drain your health, choose Battlemage after mastering Alteration and Destruction. When Aegis Bolt deals damage in ordinary PvE, it raises a shield worth twice the landed damage, up to 20% of your maximum HP. Health fell as low as 33% in the tested melee-pressure fights.
    If enemy spellcasters keep landing spells, choose Spellbreaker after mastering Destruction and Mysticism. Unravel deals damage and rolls separately for Silence. A damaging hit returns one third of the spell points paid, subject to your pool limit. Health fell as low as 91% in the tested enemy-spellcaster fights.
    Unlocking a path and acquiring its signature spell are separate steps. Buy the spell only when a funded opportunity is available.

[ ] Decisions you can postpone
    Keep your mastery and specialization slots open while practicing the required skills.
    Wait to buy an upgrade or a Spell Maker recipe until you have access and enough gold.

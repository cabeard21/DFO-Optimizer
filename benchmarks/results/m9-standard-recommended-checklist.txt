DFO class · Khajiit
Character creation and development checklist
The development example uses paired birth seed 42.

[ ] Choose your race
    Select Khajiit in character creation. Race is a separate choice when importing the class file.
    The online creator goes directly from class to name.
    Select Average reflexes to match the tested character.

[ ] Set your class attributes
    Strength 30, Intelligence 70, Willpower 75, Agility 75, Endurance 70, Personality 10, Speed 40, Luck 30.
    Set class health to 30 HP per level. Birth rolls and bonuses determine your starting sheet.

[ ] Enter the ordered skill groups
    Primary skills in order — Restoration → Destruction → Alteration
    Major skills in order — Long Blade → Mysticism → Thaumaturgy
    Minor skills in order — Orcish → Daedric → Hand-to-Hand → Climbing → Lockpicking → Illusion

[ ] Select advantages and disadvantages
    Advantages — Acute hearing, Bonus to hit Animals, Expertise in Axe, Expertise in Blunt weapon, Expertise in Hand to hand, Expertise in Long blade, Increased magery Int in spell points3.
    Disadvantages — Forbidden armor type Leather, Forbidden material Adamantium, Forbidden shield types Tower shield.
    Magery gives this class 3 spell points per point of Intelligence before other modifiers.

[ ] Spend your birth bonus
    Allocate up to four points to Endurance, then Intelligence, Speed and Willpower within legal caps. Explicit allocations override that rule.
    Spend all six points per group on its lowest rolled skills.
    The replay used Intelligence +9, Endurance +4.
    The primary bonus on this example roll went to Restoration +3, Destruction +3.
    The major bonus on this example roll went to Long Blade +2, Mysticism +1, Thaumaturgy +3.
    The minor bonus on this example roll went to Orcish +2, Daedric +1, Hand-to-Hand +2, Climbing +1.

[ ] Play around each disadvantage
    Forbidden armor type · Leather. You cannot wear leather armor.
    Forbidden material · Adamantium. You cannot equip items made of adamantium. Check the material before buying an upgrade.
    Forbidden shield types · Tower shield. You cannot equip the tower shield shield type.

[ ] Choose your opening spells
    Your starting spellbook contains Minor Shock, Arcane Arrow, Gentle Fall, Knick-Knack, Salve Bruise, Smelling Salts, Knock.
    The example birth has 237 SP available before the opening fight.
    Minor Shock costs 29 SP per cast on this roll.
    Arcane Arrow costs 28 SP per cast on this roll.
    Use Minor Shock [990] within touch range for offense.
    Use Arcane Arrow [991] at range for offense.
    Use Salve Bruise [995] to restore health. The scenario policy checks healing below 60% health.

[ ] Equip your first loadout
    Equip the starting copies used in the replay. Right hand — Broadsword, Chest clothes — Short Shirt, Legs clothes — Casual Pants, Cloak2 — Formal Cloak, Feet — Shoes.
    When spell points run low, use a proficient backup from the starting kit. The checked copies include Iron Broadsword (Long Blade).
    The carry test uses a hypothetical Ebony Cuirass. It weighs 6.25 kg.
    The starting kit plus that cuirass leaves at least 17.1 kg free across the tested births.
    The level-15 replay acquired Iron plate cuirass for 30 gold under the declared offer.

[ ] Plan your first levels
    At level-up, allocate attributes in this order. Endurance, Intelligence, Speed, Willpower, Strength, Agility, Personality, Luck.
    Practice the skills on the route, starting with Restoration, Destruction, Alteration.
    At the first earned level (2), the replay gained Endurance +5.
    The next encounter checkpoint was level 15.
    On this roll the checkpoint required 1,127,424 in-game minutes under the supplied opportunities.
    Start with the actual birth spellbook. Pay for missing practice recipes during training. At the first scored earned level, buy the stated area-touch recipe at the ordinary Spell Maker price if affordable after armor and obligatory training.
    The replay assumes one qualifying action every 60 seconds and access to full bed rests.

[ ] Plan your masteries
    Prioritize mastery in this order. Restoration, Destruction, Alteration, Mysticism, Thaumaturgy, Illusion.
    Mastered at the final checkpoint — Destruction, Restoration, Illusion, Alteration, Thaumaturgy, Mysticism.
    Primary school choices include Restoration, Destruction, Alteration.
    Major school choices include Mysticism, Thaumaturgy.
    Minor school choices include Illusion.

[ ] Choose an arcane specialization
    Leave the path open at first. The replay measured this build before choosing a specialization.
    If melee enemies repeatedly reach you and drain your health, choose Battlemage after mastering Alteration and Destruction. When Aegis Bolt deals damage in ordinary PvE, it raises a shield worth twice the landed damage, up to 20% of your maximum HP. Health fell as low as 36% in the tested melee-pressure fights.
    If enemy spellcasters keep landing spells, choose Spellbreaker after mastering Destruction and Mysticism. Unravel deals damage and rolls separately for Silence. A damaging hit returns one third of the spell points paid, subject to your pool limit. Health fell as low as 0% in the tested enemy-spellcaster fights.
    Unlocking a path and acquiring its signature spell are separate steps. Buy the spell only when a funded opportunity is available.

[ ] Decisions you can postpone
    Keep your mastery and specialization slots open while practicing the required skills.
    Wait to buy an upgrade or a Spell Maker recipe until you have access and enough gold.
